I am an undergraduate at **Tsinghua University**, where I joined the **Tsien Hsue-shen Elite Class in Mechanics** in 2024. I was a visiting student at **HKUST** in July–August 2026.

My interests span AI research and engineering, with a focus on connecting perception, reasoning, and action.

## Research Interests

- **Multimodal Reasoning** — reasoning across visual and language inputs.
- **Visual CoT** — visual chain-of-thought and grounded intermediate reasoning.
- **World Models** — learning world dynamics for prediction and planning.
- **Embodied AI / Action-Conditioned World Models** — connecting perception and action to model how environments evolve.
